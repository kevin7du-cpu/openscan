PAPERDESK SOFT 3D LIGHT
Reusable desktop appearance guide - version 1.0, 2026-11-15

HOW TO ASK FOR THIS LOOK

"Apply the PaperDesk Soft 3D Light appearance from this guide. Use a white
workspace, gently raised buttons, inset white text fields, thin beveled
dividers, subtle blue-gray shadows, and blue selection and focus accents.
Preserve this application's existing icons, control locations, wording,
functionality, and accepted interface scale. Keep the result compact and
readable. Implement the style through shared reusable components, then
build it and inspect the real running application."

Use this name together with this file in future requests. The name identifies
this custom PaperDesk appearance; it is not a built-in Slint, Windows, Fluent,
or egui theme that another application will automatically recognize.

VISUAL CHARACTER

A clean, white desktop interface with restrained depth. Controls have a
slightly raised or inset surface, delicate highlights, fine outlines, and
small soft shadows. Main content remains white and spacious; controls remain
compact. Text is crisp and comfortably readable without becoming heavy.

Use the same treatment throughout toolbars, dialogs, tabs, text fields,
dropdowns, checkboxes, radio buttons, sliders, scrollbars, and separators.
Selection, focus, hover, and pressed states must remain distinct.

BUTTON SURFACES

All gradients run from top to bottom:

Normal:   #FFFFFF at 0%, #F8FAFC at 48%, #EAF0F6 at 100%.
Hover:    #FFFFFF at 0%, #EEF7FF at 40%, #D9EAF9 at 100%.
Selected: #EFF7FF at 0%, #D7EAFF at 100%.
Pressed:  #D8E7F5 at 0%, #EDF5FC at 100%.
Primary:  #2786DD at 0%, #0067C0 at 100%, with white text.
Disabled: #F1F4F7, with subdued text and no raised shadow.

Use approximately 5 logical pixels of corner radius, a 1-pixel outline,
and a 2-pixel shadow blur with a 1-pixel downward offset. Add a fine highlight
near the upper edge. A pressed button uses a slight inset shadow instead.
Keyboard focus has a visible 2-pixel blue outline. Selected tabs can use
the pale-blue surface; inactive flat tabs remain unobtrusive.

TEXT FIELDS AND DROPDOWNS

Keep the interior white and text dark. Use a 1-pixel blue-gray outline,
approximately 4-5 pixels of corner radius, and a subtle inset edge with
roughly 3 pixels of blur and a 1-pixel downward offset. Focus uses the
blue outline. Dropdowns retain a clear arrow and align with related fields.

Render highlights and shadows around the edges or behind the editor.
Do not paint a translucent highlight across the text, placeholder, selection,
caret, or dropdown arrow. Preserve native editing, selection, clipboard,
keyboard navigation, disabled states, and accessibility behavior.

CHECKBOXES AND RADIO BUTTONS

Unchecked controls have white interiors, fine gray outlines, and a slight
inset edge. Checked controls use the blue primary gradient with a white
checkmark or white center dot. The reference indicator is 18 by 18 logical
pixels. A checkbox has softly rounded corners; a radio button is circular.
Make the accompanying label clickable and keep keyboard focus visible.

SLIDERS, SCROLLBARS, AND LINES

Retain the application's existing slider thumb and arrow shapes. Add only
subtle surface highlights and edge depth. Scrollbars should be visible and
easy to locate, with light tracks and a blue-gray raised thumb. PaperDesk's
workspace and folder scrollbars are separate and approximately 6 pixels wide;
their pointer hit areas may be larger than the visible bar.

Use a restrained two-tone bevel for separators: a blue-gray edge beside
a light highlight, about 2 pixels total. Preserve resize behavior and
adequate draggable hit areas. Do not turn dividers into large dark bands.

PANELS AND DIALOGS

Keep navigation, file lists, document surfaces, and metadata panels white.
Use thin separators to establish structure. Selected rows use the pale-blue
gradient. Dialogs may use a 6-8 pixel radius and a soft shadow, approximately
16-20 pixels of blur with a 5-6 pixel downward offset. Content and all primary
actions should fit at the normal window size; use scrolling when necessary.

SIZES AND TYPOGRAPHY

Measurements are logical pixels at 100% interface scale, not physical screen
pixels. Respect operating-system DPI and the application's chosen scale.

Reference defaults:
- Body and control text: 14 pixels, with a light-to-regular visual weight.
- PaperDesk library font: the existing licensed Ubuntu Light face.
- Scanner font: the existing licensed Inter Medium face at 15.4 pixels,
  preserving the accepted 110% scanner text scale.
- Small secondary labels: approximately 11 pixels.
- Application title: approximately 21 pixels.
- Normal controls: approximately 28 pixels high, with compact 6-8 pixel gaps.
- Dialog action buttons: approximately 30-34 pixels high.
- Text button horizontal padding: approximately 9 pixels per side.

For another application, preserve its approved font unless a font change is
requested. Use its licensed regular or medium face to achieve comparable
readability. Do not make every label semibold or bold. Do not shrink readable
text just to force a layout to fit; adjust spacing, wrapping, or scrolling.

ICONS AND LAYOUT

Leave existing icon files, colors, shapes, sizes, and meanings unchanged.
This theme does not require replacing icons. PaperDesk uses its existing
Fluent assets and original vector symbols; the theme adds depth around them.

Preserve the target application's layout and behavior. Applying this look
does not mean replacing every application's layout with PaperDesk's panels.
Keep natural button widths and aligned form columns. Prevent single-line
fields from stretching into large empty boxes. Wrap long paths and labels.

For document applications, show the top of a newly selected page, preserve
its aspect ratio, provide scroll extents for the whole page, and support
zoom without clipping content. Keep preview and metadata actions accessible.

IMPLEMENTATION AND CHECKING

Put colors, gradients, borders, radii, and shadows in shared theme tokens.
Apply them through reusable controls rather than styling every screen
independently. Keep application logic separate from appearance code.

The Slint reference implementation is in PaperDesk Slint's ui/surfaces.slint,
ui/widgets.slint, ui/scan-controls.slint, and ui/scanner.slint, with layout in
ui/app.slint. It layers custom styling over the supplied modern-theme.slint;
copying modern-theme.slint alone will not reproduce this appearance.

The visual rules can be implemented in Slint or another UI toolkit. Exact
font metrics and shadow rendering vary, so inspect the running application
at its normal size, smaller sizes, and supported scaling. Check text contrast,
overflow, scrolling, dropdowns, focus, editing, hover, pressed states, and
disabled states. Preserve the relevant licenses when reusing code or assets.

This guide describes the approved light appearance. A dark variant should
be designed and checked separately rather than produced by color inversion.
