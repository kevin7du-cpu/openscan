# OpenScan Rust port — ver0.2.7

The independent workspace contains the core, optional egui panel and shared office widgets; it does not depend on PaperDesk. Start with [README](README.md), [migration](docs/MIGRATION.md), [API](docs/API.md), [changes](CHANGELOG.md), [scan quality](crates/scan-module/docs/SCAN_QUALITY.md), and [validation](docs/VALIDATION.md).

The framework is eframe with egui and wgpu. No C#, .NET, NuGet or OpenCvSharp is required. Original licensing and historical notices are retained; current dependency/model/font licenses are recorded separately.
